🛡️ FASTATION Privacy Policy


1. Introduction

At FASTATION, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide while using the app.

2. Information We Collect

FASTATION collects the following data when you register or use the app:
	•	Full name
	•	Phone number
	•	Email address
	•	Geographic location
	•	Uploaded files (if any)
	•	Usage data (such as activity within the app and entered locations)

3. How We Use the Information

We use your data to:
	•	Create and manage your user account
	•	Improve app performance and user experience
	•	Organize and optimize field trips efficiently
	•	Communicate with you when necessary (technical support, updates, or notifications)

4. Information Sharing

FASTATION may share certain data only when necessary, such as with:
	•	Mapping services (e.g., Google Maps)
	•	Hosting and analytics services
	•	Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no online system can be 100% secure.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:
	•	Access and update your personal data
	•	Request deletion of your account and data
	•	Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of any major changes through the app or via email.
